LiquidGlass Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 16, 2026

LiquidGlass ("the App") is made by an independent developer. This document explains what information the App accesses, how it is used, and what it does not do.

Motion Sensor

The App reads your device's accelerometer so the simulated liquid reacts when you tilt or shake your phone. The sensor readings are only used on your device, in real time, to drive the simulation — they are never recorded, saved, or uploaded anywhere. The App requires no special permission for this.

Settings

The App remembers which hourglass you last chose. This setting is stored only on your device.

Advertising (Google AdMob)

The App is free to use and supported by ads, currently served through Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, AdMob and its advertising partners may collect:

  • Resettable advertising identifiers
  • Approximate location (e.g. city-level, not precise GPS)
  • Device information (model, OS version, etc.)
  • IP address

This data is handled by Google under its own privacy policy; the App's developer does not separately collect or retain it. See:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google AdMob uses data

Network Access

The App requests network permissions to load ads and to check whether an internet connection is available (showing a prompt if it is not). This check happens entirely on your device and is never sent to any server. The hourglass simulation itself works entirely on your device.

Accounts & Sign-in

The App does not require an account and does not collect your name, email address, or password.

Data Sharing

Other than the AdMob data described above, which Google handles under its own policy, the developer does not sell or share your data with any third party.

Children's Privacy

The App is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Changes to This Policy

If the App adds new advertising services or features that change how data is collected, this policy will be updated accordingly, along with the date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to: lyenliang@gmail.com

液態沙漏（LiquidGlass）隱私權政策

最後更新日期：2026 年 10 月 16 日

液態沙漏（LiquidGlass，以下稱「本 App」）由個人開發者製作。這份文件說明本 App 會接觸到哪些資訊、怎麼使用，以及不會做什麼。

動作感應器

本 App 會讀取手機的加速度感應器，讓模擬的液體在你傾斜或搖動手機時跟著反應。感應器數值只在你的裝置上即時用來計算模擬，不會被記錄、儲存，也不會上傳到任何地方。這項功能不需要任何特殊權限。

設定

本 App 會記住你上次選擇的沙漏種類，這項設定只儲存在你的裝置上。

廣告（Google AdMob）

本 App 免費提供，透過顯示廣告維持運作，廣告服務目前由 Google AdMob 提供。為了顯示和衡量廣告成效，AdMob 及其合作的廣告商可能會收集：

  • 裝置的廣告識別碼（可重設）
  • 大概的地理位置（例如城市層級，非精確 GPS 座標）
  • 裝置資訊（型號、作業系統版本等）
  • IP 位址

這些資料由 Google 依照其自身的隱私權政策處理，本 App 開發者不會另外取得或保存這些資料。詳情請參考：

  • Google 隱私權政策
  • Google AdMob 如何使用資料

網路連線

本 App 需要網路權限來載入廣告，以及偵測目前有沒有網路連線（沒有網路時會提示你開啟網路才能繼續使用）。這個偵測完全在你的裝置上進行，不會把偵測結果傳送給任何伺服器。沙漏模擬本身也完全在你的裝置上執行。

帳號與登入

本 App 不需要註冊帳號，也不會向你收集姓名、電子郵件、密碼等個人資料。

資料分享

除了上述 AdMob 廣告服務依其自身政策收集的資料之外，本 App 開發者不會將你的任何資料出售或分享給其他第三方。

兒童隱私

本 App 並非特別針對 13 歲以下兒童設計，開發者不會刻意收集兒童的個人資料。

政策異動

如果本 App 未來新增其他廣告服務或功能而影響資料蒐集方式，這份政策會一併更新，並修改上方的最後更新日期。
